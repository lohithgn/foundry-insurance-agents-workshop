## Section 2 — Covered Losses

### 2.1 Liability (Coverage A)
Pays for bodily injury and property damage to others for which an insured is legally responsible arising from the ownership or use of the insured vehicle.

### 2.2 Collision (Coverage D)
Pays for direct damage to the insured vehicle caused by **impact with another vehicle or object**, or by **upset (rollover)**, regardless of fault. Subject to the $500 Collision deductible.

### 2.3 Comprehensive / Other Than Collision (Coverage D)
Pays for direct damage to the insured vehicle caused by perils **other than** collision, including **theft, fire, vandalism, hail, falling objects, flood, and animal strikes** (e.g., hitting a deer). Subject to the $250 Comprehensive deductible.

### 2.4 Glass
Windshield and window glass repair is covered under Comprehensive. The deductible is **waived for repair** (chip/crack) but applies to full glass **replacement**.

## Section 3 — Exclusions

This policy does not cover:
1. Damage while the vehicle is used for **commercial ride-share or delivery** without the ride-share endorsement.
2. Loss due to **racing** or speed contests.
3. **Intentional** damage caused by an insured.
4. Damage from **wear and tear**, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or freezing.
5. Loss while the vehicle is driven by a person **excluded** on the policy or without a valid license.
6. **Custom equipment** beyond a $1,000 sublimit unless scheduled.

---

## Section 4 — Claims Process

1. Report the claim within 30 days; for theft or vandalism, file a **police report** and provide the report number.
2. Provide the **date, time, and location** of the incident and a description of how it happened.
3. Provide **photographs** of the damage and the other party's insurance/contact details if applicable.
4. Obtain a repair estimate from an approved shop; the deductible is subtracted from the covered amount.
