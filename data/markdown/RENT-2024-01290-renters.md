## Section 2 — Covered Perils (Named Perils)

Personal property is covered against direct loss caused by these named perils only:
1. Fire and lightning
2. Smoke
3. Windstorm and hail
4. Theft (police report required)
5. Vandalism and malicious mischief
6. **Sudden and accidental water discharge** from plumbing or appliances (e.g., a burst pipe in the unit). Resulting damage to belongings is covered.
7. Falling objects
8. Weight of ice, snow, or sleet

---

## Section 3 — Exclusions

1. **Flood** and surface water (excluded).
2. **Earth movement** (earthquake, landslide).
3. **Wear and tear**, gradual deterioration, mold from long-term humidity.
4. **The building or fixtures** owned by the landlord.
5. **Pest damage** (insects, rodents).
6. **Business inventory** beyond a $1,500 sublimit.

---

## Section 4 — Claims Process

1. Notify Northwind Mutual within 30 days.
2. Provide an itemized inventory of damaged or stolen belongings with approximate values and proof of ownership where possible.
3. Provide photographs; for theft, provide a police report number.
4. The $500 deductible is subtracted from each covered loss; personal property is settled at Actual Cash Value.
